# Privacy Policy — DuoFilm

Effective date: 2026-11-17

DuoFilm is an Android camera application developed by geoclest.

## Data collection

DuoFilm collects **no personal data** and transmits **no data off your device**.
The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no network communication
with the developer or any third party.

Specifically:

- **Camera recordings** (the two video streams DuoFilm records) are written only
  to storage that belongs to the app on your device. They are never uploaded.
- **Microphone audio**, when the microphone permission is granted, is encoded and
  muxed into the video files on your device only. It is never transmitted.
- **Optional detailed local logs**, if you enable them in the app's settings, are
  stored in bounded rotating files in app-private storage on your device. They can
  be cleared or exported by you from within the app, and there is no upload path.
- **Model files** used by the optional offline enhancement are stored inside the
  app and run entirely on-device.

## Permissions

DuoFilm requests only the permissions it needs to function:

- Camera — to record the two video streams.
- Microphone — optional; used only if you grant it, and only for the audio track
  of your recordings. You can mute with the in-app HUD button at any time.
- Storage — limited to the app's own media directory for saving recordings and
  the optional local log files.

## Data deletion

Deleting DuoFilm (or using "Clear data" in Android settings) removes all videos,
logs, and app data. You can also delete individual recordings through your
device's gallery or the in-app gallery link.

## Children

DuoFilm is intended for a general audience aged 18 and over and is not directed
at children.
